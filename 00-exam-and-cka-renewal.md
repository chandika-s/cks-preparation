# Exam facts & your CKA question

## CKS exam format (official, training.linuxfoundation.org)

- Online, proctored, performance-based — command-line tasks against live clusters, no multiple choice.
- Duration: **2 hours**.
- Currently based on **Kubernetes v1.35**.
- Two exam attempts included with registration; 12 months eligibility window to schedule/take it.
- Prerequisite: you must have a **passed CKA** (active or not — see below) before you can sit the CKS exam.
- Includes 2 killer.sh simulator attempts (36 hours access each, 17 questions per session) — use these in Week 7/8, they're the closest thing to the real environment.
- Certification validity: **2 years** from the day you pass.

## Does passing CKS extend your CKA? — Yes, in your case

Under the Linux Foundation's **CARE program** (Certification Automatic Renewal/Extension):

- As of a policy expansion, **earning or recertifying CKS on or after June 18, 2026 automatically extends your CKA** — your CKA expiration date is updated to match the new CKS expiration date.
- This applies **whether your CKA is still active or already expired** — you don't need an active CKA for the extension to kick in, only a passed CKA is required as the CKS prerequisite in the first place.
- It is **not retroactive** for CKS certifications earned before June 18, 2026.

**What this means for you:** your CKA expires Dec 19, 2026, and you're targeting the CKS exam by Nov 15, 2026 — after the June 18, 2026 cutoff. If you pass CKS on Nov 15, 2026, your CKA expiration will automatically move out to align with your new CKS expiration (2 years from your CKS pass date), so you won't need a separate CKA renewal in December.
