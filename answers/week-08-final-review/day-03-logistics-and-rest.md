## Why it works
- Logistics failures (ID mismatch, camera, browser) are the most avoidable way to lose an attempt or waste minutes of the timer.
- Sleep improves recall and speed more than another lab at this stage.

## Common mistakes / exam gotchas
- ID name differs from registration (middle name, order).
- Second monitor or virtual display left connected.
- OS update or VPN/corporate proxy interfering with the secure browser; use a personal, unmanaged machine and network if the handbook requires it.
- Late-night cramming.
- Forgetting the time zone conversion.
